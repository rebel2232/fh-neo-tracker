Help & Guidance

What is the NEO Checklist Tracker?

The New Employee Orientation (NEO) Checklist Tracker is a tool that helps Fraser Health managers and administrators track health and safety orientation completion for new and young workers, in compliance with WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation §3.23.

Key Features

  • Two views: Admin view shows all employees across all facilities. Manager view filters to your direct reports by cost centre.
  • Automatic 14-day tracking: Due dates are calculated 14 days from the hire date with automatic overdue flagging.
  • Live dashboard: Click any stat card (Total, Complete, Overdue, Due Soon, Incomplete) to instantly filter the list.
  • Visual urgency cues: Overdue rows have a red border, due-soon rows have an orange border, and inline text shows days remaining or days overdue.
  • Required file upload: Submitting a completed checklist requires attaching the signed document.
  • Export: Download the full register as CSV for reporting or audits.

For Managers

  1. Switch to Manager View using the toggle in the top bar.
  2. Select your name from the dropdown — the list filters to employees in your cost centre.
  3. Review the dashboard for any Overdue or Due Soon alerts.
  4. For each pending employee, click the ✓ button to submit their completed checklist.
  5. Upload the signed checklist file and confirm the completion date.

For Admins

  1. Use Admin View to see all employees across all facilities.
  2. Filter by facility, status, or cost centre using the dropdowns.
  3. Click stat cards to quickly see overdue or due-soon employees across the organization.
  4. Use the Export button to generate a CSV for compliance reporting.

How to Submit a Completed NEO Checklist

Submission requires both a completion date and an uploaded checklist file.

Step-by-step
  1. Find the employee in the table.
  2. Click the green ✓ button in the Actions column.
  3. Enter the date the orientation was completed.
  4. Upload the signed checklist (drag & drop or click to browse).
  5. Add any notes about the orientation if needed.
  6. Click Submit Completion.

Accepted File Types

PDF, DOC, DOCX, JPG, PNG. Recommended naming: NEO_LastName_YYYY-MM-DD.pdf

⚠ Important: Once submitted, the system records this as the official completion. Contact the Health & Safety team if you need to revise a submission.

Status Definitions

✓ Complete Employee has completed orientation and a signed checklist has been uploaded to the system.
● Incomplete Orientation has not yet been completed. Within the 14-day window, more than 3 days remain.
◉ Due Soon Within 3 days of the 14-day deadline. Action recommended.
⚠ Overdue More than 14 days have passed since the hire date and no checklist has been submitted. Compliance gap — immediate action required.
— Not Required NEO is not applicable for this employee (e.g., volunteer, contractor, or returning staff).

WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation §3.23

Young or new worker orientation and training
An employer must ensure that before a young or new worker begins work in a workplace, the young or new worker is given health and safety orientation and training specific to that young or new worker's workplace.

Required Topics (3.23(2))

Orientation and training must include the following:

  • The name and contact information of the young or new worker's supervisor
  • The employer's and worker's rights and responsibilities under the Workers Compensation Act and OHS Regulation, including the right to refuse unsafe work
  • Workplace health and safety rules
  • Hazards to which the young or new worker may be exposed, including risks from robbery, assault, or confrontation
  • Working alone or in isolation procedures
  • Violence in the workplace policies and procedures
  • Personal protective equipment requirements
  • Location of first aid facilities, means of summoning first aid, and reporting injuries
  • Emergency procedures, including evacuation and assembly points
  • Instruction and demonstration of the young or new worker's work task or work process
  • The employer's health and safety program (if any)
  • WHMIS information
  • Contact information for the joint health and safety committee or worker health and safety representative

Definitions

  • Young worker: A worker who is under 25 years of age.
  • New worker: A worker new to a workplace, returning after a substantial break, affected by changes in hazards, or relocated to a new workplace.

For the full regulation text, visit the WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation Part 3.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the deadline 14 days from hire?
Fraser Health policy aligns with WorkSafeBC §3.23 which requires orientation before the worker begins work. The 14-day window provides a tracking buffer for documentation and follow-up while remaining compliant.
What if the orientation was completed before this system was set up?
Submit the historical date the orientation was completed and upload the signed checklist as it was originally documented. The system will record the completion against the historical date.
My employee transferred from another department — do they need a new NEO?
Yes. Per §3.23, a worker is considered "new" when relocated to a workplace where the work or hazards differ from their previous workplace. A site-specific orientation is required.
Can I submit on behalf of another manager?
Admins can submit for any employee. Managers should only submit for employees within their cost centre.
What happens to overdue items?
Overdue items are flagged in red and counted in the compliance banner. Managers should prioritize completing overdue orientations immediately and document any reasons for delay.
Can I change a submission after it's been recorded?
No — submissions are final once recorded to maintain audit integrity. If a correction is needed, contact the Health & Safety team or your local site administrator.
Where does the employee data come from?
Employee records are sourced from the Fraser Health HR system. New hires automatically appear in the manager's list within 24 hours of being added to HR records.
Who do I contact for help?
For technical issues, contact the IT Service Desk. For policy or compliance questions, contact your Workplace Health & Safety advisor or email safety@fraserhealth.ca.

What is the NEO Checklist Tracker?

The New Employee Orientation (NEO) Checklist Tracker helps Fraser Health managers track health and safety orientation completion for new and young workers, in compliance with WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation §3.23.

Key Features

  • Export: Download the full register as CSV for compliance reporting.

How to Confirm a Completed NEO Checklist

Confirming completion requires both the completion date and an uploaded checklist file.

Step-by-step
  1. Find the employee in the Employee NEO Register.
  2. In the Status column, click the blue Confirm Completion link.
  3. Enter the date the orientation was completed.
  4. Upload the signed checklist (drag & drop or click to browse).
  5. Add any notes about the orientation if needed.
  6. Click Confirm Completion to save.

Accepted File Types

PDF, DOC, DOCX, JPG, PNG. Recommended naming: NEO_LastName_YYYY-MM-DD.pdf

⚠ Important: Once confirmed, the system records this as the official completion. Use the Revise option if a correction is needed.

How to Revise a Completed Submission

If you confirmed a completion in error or need to update the details, the revise feature lets you correct the record. All revisions are audit-tracked.

Two Revision Options

  • Correct details only — fix a wrong date or replace the uploaded file. The submission remains marked Complete.
  • Revert to incomplete — undo the submission entirely. The 14-day clock resumes from the hire date, and the row may immediately flag as Overdue if too much time has passed.
Step-by-step
  1. Find the completed employee in the register.
  2. Click the orange ↺ icon in the Revise Submission column.
  3. Review the current submission details and the audit-tracked warning.
  4. Select either Correct details only or Revert to incomplete.
  5. Tick the acknowledgment checkbox confirming you understand the change is logged.
  6. Provide a reason for the revision (required for audit purposes).
  7. For Revert, confirm a second time on the red warning panel.
⚠ All revisions are permanently logged with timestamp, previous values, new values, and the reason provided. This protects audit integrity.
