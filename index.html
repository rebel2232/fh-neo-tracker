Help & Guidance

What is the NEO Checklist Tracker?

The New Employee Orientation (NEO) Checklist Tracker is a centralized register that helps Fraser Health leaders track health and safety orientation completion for new and young workers, in compliance with WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation §3.23.

Key Features

  • Automatic 14-day tracking: Due dates are calculated 14 days from the hire date, with automatic overdue flagging.
  • Live dashboard: Three clickable summary tiles at the top of the page — Total Employees, NEO Complete, and Action Required. Click any tile to filter the register.
  • Visual urgency cues: Overdue rows have a red left border, due-soon rows have an orange left border, and inactive employees appear greyed-out.
  • Required file upload: Confirming completion requires attaching the completed checklist file.
  • File Attachment column: Uploaded checklists appear as clickable links.
  • Notes column: Leaders can add contextual notes about each employee's NEO status.
  • Revisable submissions: Completed entries can be corrected with mandatory audit reason.
  • Pre-System Complete: Employees hired before June 1, 2026 are auto-acknowledged as legacy hires.
  • Filters: Leader, facility, cost centre, NEO status, and employment status filters.
  • Delegate Access: Grant full access to another user to manage your dashboard.
  • Export: Download the full register as CSV.

For Leaders

  1. Review the dashboard for any Overdue or Due Soon alerts.
  2. For each pending employee, click the Confirm Completion button to submit their completed checklist.
  3. Upload the completed checklist file and confirm the completion date.

How to Submit a Completed NEO Checklist

Submission requires both a completion date and an uploaded checklist file.

Step-by-step
  1. Find the employee in the table.
  2. Click the Confirm Completion button.
  3. Enter the date the orientation was completed.
  4. Upload the completed checklist file (drag & drop or click to browse).
  5. Add any notes about the orientation if needed.
  6. Click Confirm Completion.

Accepted File Types

PDF, DOC, DOCX, JPG, PNG. Recommended naming: NEO_LastName_YYYY-MM-DD.pdf

Status Definitions

✓ CompleteEmployee has completed orientation and the checklist file has been uploaded.
● IncompleteOrientation not yet confirmed. Within the 14-day window with more than 3 days remaining.
◉ Due SoonWithin 3 days of the 14-day deadline. Orange border highlights the row.
⚠ OverduePast the 14-day window without confirmation. Red border. Immediate action required.
⏳ Pre-System CompleteAutomatic acknowledgment for employees hired before June 1, 2026. Their orientation is presumed completed under prior process. Managers can still use Confirm Completion to promote the record to a verified ✓ Complete.

WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation §3.23

Young or new worker orientation and training
An employer must ensure that before a young or new worker begins work, they are given health and safety orientation and training specific to that worker's workplace.

Required Topics (3.23(2))

  • Supervisor contact info; rights and responsibilities under the Workers Compensation Act; workplace H&S rules; hazards; working alone procedures; violence prevention; PPE; first aid; emergency procedures; work task instruction; H&S program; WHMIS; JOHSC contact.
What happens to employees hired before June 1, 2026?
Auto-classified as ⏳ Pre-System Complete. Leaders can still confirm completion to promote to verified ✓ Complete.
How do I retain records for departed employees?
Set employment status to Inactive. Use the filter to view Active, Inactive, or All.
Where can I see uploaded checklist files?
In the File Attachment column. Click the 📄 link to open.
Why is the deadline 14 days from hire?
Aligns with §3.23. The 14-day window provides a documentation buffer while remaining compliant.
Can I change a submission after it's been confirmed?
Yes — use the orange ↺ Revise icon. All changes are audit-tracked.
What should I put in the Notes column?
Scheduling updates, follow-up dates, delayed completion reasons. NOT HR issues, disciplinary matters, medical info, or performance concerns.
Who do I contact for help?
Technical issues: IT Service Desk. Policy questions: safety@fraserhealth.ca.
